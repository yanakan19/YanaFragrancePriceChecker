    What the code guarantees

    Every figure on this page comes from the committed modules, bundled unchanged — the demo has no pricing logic of its own, so it cannot drift from what ships.

    Delivered price is the default sort

    Delivery routinely exceeds the price gap, and thresholds run from £25 to £300. Switch to item price and watch Dior Sauvage reorder — Boots is cheapest per bottle and dearest delivered.

    Discounts are the retailer's, never ours

    A was/now pair is drawn only when the retailer published a reference price above what it charges. Percentages round down — 19.6% shows as 19%.

    No invented countdowns

    A timer appears only against a retailer-published end time. Where none exists the row says so. A fabricated deadline is pressure selling, and an ASA problem.

    Membership rates are never the headline

    Beautycard, Selfridges+, MYTFS and Premier all beat the modelled rate. None are priced in — quoting a members-only price to a non-member shows a number they cannot pay.

    Unavailable is never cheapest

    Explicit out-of-stock rows sink to the bottom and can never be the headline. unknown ranks between: a page we failed to parse is not proof the product is gone.

    Ranking is never for sale

    Order depends on stock and price only. No affiliate programme is live yet, so every link here is a plain retailer URL — and commission must never move a row.
