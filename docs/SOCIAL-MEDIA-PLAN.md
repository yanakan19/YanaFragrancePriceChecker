# PriceSniffs social media plan

Written 2026-10-01. A plan you can follow from top to bottom: which accounts
to create, what to post, how often, and the UK rules that apply. For the
TikTok Shop affiliate integration specifically, see
[TIKTOK-SHOP-PLAN.md](TIKTOK-SHOP-PLAN.md) §6, which this document does not
repeat.

---

## 1. The short version

- **Two names, used consistently everywhere.**
  - **@yannysniffs** is *you*: the face, the voice, the person sniffing and
    talking. Already live on TikTok and Instagram, and linked from the site's
    footer and About page.
  - **@pricesniffs** is *the site*: deal alerts, price drops, links. Grab it
    on every platform below today, even the ones you will not post on yet,
    so nobody else can.
- **Lead with TikTok, then Instagram.** That is where UK fragrance buyers
  are. Everything else repurposes what you make there.
- **Your unfair advantage is data nobody else has.** The site tracks 25,000+
  live offers across 33 UK shops, keeps price history, and checks every
  "was £X" claim against other shops. Most deal accounts just repost shop
  banners. You can say *"this deal is real"* or *"this 'deal' is fake, it's
  been that price for weeks"*, and prove it.

## 3. What to post: five content pillars

Each pillar uses something the site already knows, so you are never stuck
for ideas.

1. **"Real deal or fake deal?"** (your signature series). Take a deal a shop
   is shouting about. The site's reference-price check shows whether the
   "was £X" is backed by other shops (3,748 corroborated, 380 refuted in the
   last build). Show the price history. Verdict in 3 seconds. This is the
   content nobody else can make.
2. **"Cheapest place in the UK for ___ today."** Take a bestseller (Sauvage,
   Bleu de Chanel, Lattafa Khamrah, Club de Nuit Intense) and screen-record
   the product page on the site, sorted by delivered price. Include the
   delivery cost; that is where most "cheap" prices stop being cheap.
3. **Dupes, priced.** The original vs its Middle Eastern dupe, both priced
   live. "£120 vs £24: here's the difference." Huge on FragranceTok.
4. **"Don't buy it this week."** A price history chart showing a bottle that
   is currently above its usual price: "it was £X less 3 weeks ago, wait."
   Trust-building, and it makes followers come back.
5. **Yanny sniffs** (personality). Blind sniff tests, first impressions,
   "smells expensive, costs £18". This is what makes people follow a person
   rather than a deal bot.

**Formats that work:** a face-to-camera hook in the first second ("This
'50% off' is fake"), screen recordings of the site, green-screen over the
price chart, and carousels (Instagram, Lemon8, Pinterest) of "5 real deals
this week".

## 5. UK advertising rules (these apply to you)

UK rules are enforced by the ASA (advertising) and the CMA (consumer law),
and both have acted against influencers.

- **Affiliate links are adverts.** When a post links to, or shows, a product
  you earn commission on, label it clearly **at the start**: "#ad" or "Ad".
  "#affiliate", "#sp" or "link in bio" alone is not enough (ASA/CAP
  *Influencers' guide*).
- **Use each platform's own label as well**: TikTok's "Branded content"
  toggle, Instagram's "Paid partnership" label for brand deals, and TikTok
  Shop's own product-link labelling.
- **Gifted products count too.** If a shop or brand sends you a bottle, say
  so ("gifted").
- **Price claims must be true on the day.** "Cheapest in the UK" needs to
  be what the site shows at the time of posting. Say "cheapest of the 33
  shops we check, today". The site's own wording is careful about this, so
  keep the posts the same.
- **No fake urgency.** Do not say "ends tonight" unless you know it does.
- **Use your own photos and videos.** Shop product photos belong to the
  shop or brand.

## 6. Using the site to run the deal accounts (later, optional)

The site already builds a list of 1,796 current deals with verified
reference prices (`demo/deals.generated.ts`), refreshed by every crawl. A
small daily job could turn the top 3–5 into:

- a post on X, Threads and Telegram (text plus link), and
- a WhatsApp Channel message (copy-paste, about a minute; WhatsApp Channels
  had no public posting API at the time of writing),
- a weekly newsletter draft.

This is a good next build once the accounts exist. Ask for it as
"automated daily deal posts from the deals list", and it can reuse the
crawl's existing schedule. Keep TikTok and Instagram human: automated
posting there gets throttled and reads as spam.

## 8. What to measure (monthly, 15 minutes)

| Measure | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Followers per platform | each app | Growth, and when you hit 1,000 on TikTok |
| Average watch time / completion | TikTok & Instagram analytics | The number the algorithms reward |
| Visits by `utm_source` | site analytics (add a free, privacy-friendly one, e.g. Cloudflare Web Analytics) | Which platform actually sends buyers |
| Affiliate clicks and earnings | Awin, TikTok Shop, Amazon dashboards | Whether any of it pays |
| Newsletter and WhatsApp subscribers | Beehiiv/Buttondown, WhatsApp | Audience you own |

Stop doing whatever does not move any of these after 60 days.
