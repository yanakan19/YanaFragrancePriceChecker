How entries were accepted:

- A search result had to be the shop's own Trustpilot review page, and its title or snippet had to name the shop.
- The domain in the path had to be the shop's registry domain, or clearly the same site or brand (with or without www, or a parent or sub domain).
- Where only a different business with a similar name turned up, the domain differed, or nothing was found, the cell says "not found" and nothing is stored in the registry.
- Recorded form is `https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/<domain>`. In most cases the search result was the same path on another locale (au., ie., dk., ...) or a `?page=N` page of it, so the uk. base address was written from that path rather than copied from a uk. result. Rows whose result was exactly the uk. address say "(exact)". If you want to be strict, strike the rows that are not "(exact)" and check them by hand.
- Review counts and scores that appeared in result titles are replaced with "[count omitted]". No scores, ratings or review counts are recorded anywhere.
