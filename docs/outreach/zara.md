# Zara: permission request (draft, not sent)

Drafted 2026-10-03, refreshed 2026-10-08 for the owner. Nothing has been sent.

## Is it worth sending? Low priority

Zara has no open affiliate programme and no product feed for publishers:
what exists is an invitation only creator "Ambassador" scheme (reported to
run through LTK for selected influencers), which is not a price feed. So the
only lawful route is this permission email.

Zara also sells only its own perfumes (`singleBrandOnly: 'Zara'` in the
registry), so no other shop's price would ever sit beside its listings on
PriceSniffs. Send this after the affiliate applications for The Fragrance
Shop, The Perfume Shop, Selfridges and Harvey Nichols, if at all.

## Where to send it

**Best route found:** none confirmed on the shop's own site.

zara.com refuses our requests (its robots.txt answered 403 Access Denied on 2026-10-03), and a web search did not return a UK contact, press or partnerships page on zara.com. Open https://www.zara.com/uk/ in an ordinary browser and use the contact option in its Help section.

## The email

Replace [YOUR NAME] before sending.

    Subject: Permission to show Zara prices on PriceSniffs

    Hello,

    My name is [YOUR NAME] and I run PriceSniffs (https://pricesniffs.space), a free UK price comparison site for fragrance.

    PriceSniffs shows what a bottle costs at each UK shop that sells it, with delivery included, so a shopper sees the real total before they click. Every listing links straight to the shop's own product page, where the sale happens. We never change a shop's price, and if we cannot keep a price current we take it off the site rather than show an old one.

    We would like to include Zara's fragrances. At the moment our checks of your public pages, robots.txt included, are turned away by your website's security ("Access Denied"), and we respect that, so we have stopped trying.

    Would you be willing to let us read your public perfume prices, for example by allowing our crawler (it identifies itself as PriceSniffsBot and follows robots.txt) to visit your two perfume pages at a gentle rate? Or, if you prefer, would you share a product data feed with prices, stock and product links? Either would let us send shoppers to you with accurate, current prices.

    Whichever suits you, we will follow any conditions you set, such as how often we check or which pages we use. I would be glad to answer any questions.

    Kind regards,

    [YOUR NAME]
