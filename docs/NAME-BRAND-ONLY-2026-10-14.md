# Names that are only the brand, 14 Oct 2026

Owner request: a fragrance sold as "Fcuk" is usually "Fcuk Her" or "Fcuk Him" and the listing lost the qualifier.

## Fixed

- When two records share a barcode and one is named only the brand, the product takes the full name from the other record (build-demo-catalogue.ts, applyMerges). This made the two Fcuk Original products read "Fcuk Her" and "Fcuk Him" (barcodes 0085715003713 and 0085715003720; Direct Cosmetics titles them "fcuk fcuk Her" and "fcuk fcuk Him"). They stay two products: different barcodes.
- A name that would be only an audience phrase or one generic word keeps the house ("Moncler Pour Homme", "Burberry For Women", "Police Original").
- Before: 179 UK products named only the brand. After: 172.

No shop title, barcode partner or audience field gives a qualifier. Most are the house's own eponymous fragrance (Jimmy Choo, Chloe, Aramis, Juicy Couture, Paloma Picasso). Brands whose products carry more than one audience, and so are the likeliest to be missing a Her or Him, are: none.
